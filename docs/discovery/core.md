## Reading the requirements

- **R** means a documented capability translated into a concrete product requirement.
- **C** means a conditional integration, deployment, or experimental capability. Implement the stated workflow only when that capability is offered; do not fake success when its provider/tool is absent.
- **U** means evidence does not establish the behavior. The concrete requirement is to expose a defined, bounded product contract rather than claim the undocumented guarantee. Its acceptance checks that boundary, not an invented Overleaf behavior.
- Commercial rules are reference facts, not automatically Quire requirements. Numeric limits, paid gates, publication policy, and edition names are separated below.
- Unless a row explicitly requires a timing policy, tests should subscribe to the relevant save, compile-completion, refresh, or download event before triggering it, with a bounded failure timeout. Do not test asynchronous behavior with fixed sleeps.
